Jane Street Quant Trader Preparation – Stage 1

This document focuses on building fast arithmetic, numerical intuition, and memorization for interview mental math.

1. Zetamac Strategy

  • Accuracy first (95%+) before speed.
  • Read the whole expression once before calculating.
  • Round mentally to estimate the answer, then compute exactly.
  • Skip unusually long problems if the interface allows.
  • Practice in blocks: +, −, ×, ÷ separately before mixed mode.
  • Never recalculate from scratch after a mistake—track partial results.
Mental multiplication tricks
  • (a+b)(a−b)=a²−b²
  • 49×51=(50−1)(50+1)=2500−1=2499
  • 25×x = x÷4 then ×100.
  • 11×AB = A (A+B) B (carry if needed).
  • 99×x = 100x−x.
  • 101×x = 100x+x.

7. Useful Constants

  • √2 ≈ 1.4142
  • √3 ≈ 1.7321
  • √5 ≈ 2.2361
  • π ≈ 3.14159
  • e ≈ 2.71828
  • ln(2) ≈ 0.6931
  • ln(10) ≈ 2.3026
  • log10(2) ≈ 0.3010
  • log2(10) ≈ 3.3219

8. Daily Drill (60 minutes)

  1. 15 min – Zetamac (+,−)
  2. 15 min – Multiplication and division only
  3. 10 min – Review squares, cubes, powers
  4. 10 min – Fraction/decimal recall
  5. 10 min – Mixed mental arithmetic without calculator
